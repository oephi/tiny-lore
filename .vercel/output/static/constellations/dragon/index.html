The Smallest Flame

The Dragon

✦

In the mountain where the great dragons lived, everything was fire and thunder. They roared so loud the clouds ran away. They breathed flames so high the eagles had to take detours.

All except the smallest dragon, who could only manage a flame about the size of a birthday candle.

The other dragons were kind about it, in the way that very large things are kind to very small things — gently, and from a great distance.

One cold night, a lost sparrow flew into the mountain cave, shivering and too tired to fly another wing-beat. The great dragons tried to help, but their flames were so enormous the poor bird was nearly blown away.

The smallest dragon crept forward. She breathed her tiny flame — no bigger than a matchstick — and held it steady. The sparrow hopped close, fluffed its feathers, and let out a contented chirp.

They sat together all night, the smallest dragon and the smallest bird, sharing the smallest fire in the mountain. And it was, by any measure, exactly enough.
