In tiny lore, there is a world that has no walls. 

This might sound strange. 

Most places come with edges. Like doors, gates, windows or corners. 

But in this world, there are none. 

This world is entirely different: There are no borders, no barriers, no hard shapes. 

At first, it can seem like there is nothing here. 

But…there is so much more than that. 

The more you spend time in this world, you come to realise that this world is in fact, full of air – endless, gentle, everywhere all at once, holding everything - including you. 

In this world, you don’t have to go anywhere. Or do anything. 

Instead, the air takes you. You do not even need to open your eyes. 

All you need to do is float. Just float. 

And as you float, notice what passes by. Big things. Small things. Quiet things. Things with sound. Things that shimmer. Things that sparkle. 

And all you are doing is floating. 

Just floating. 

In this world, there is no line telling you where you end and everything else begins. There are no walls. No hard edges. 

Only softness remains as you float, held like a giant cloud in the space. 

We call this world: The Gentle Place.
